Case study coming once the app ships. So far: a notch that plays along (cover on one side, a waveform on the
other), opens into a player on hover, tucks behind the camera when nothing is playing, and shows time-synced
lyrics on the lock screen. Next up: multi-monitor support and accent colours, then a settings window.
